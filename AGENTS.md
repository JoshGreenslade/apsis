# Curriculum and prose changes

Read docs/CURRICULUM_AUTHORING.md before editing course content or its schema.
The original prose was too dense; do not restore that density while restoring
useful material. Write naturally, with enough space for a reader to follow the
argument. Avoid applying a fixed rhetorical pattern to every lesson or paragraph.

Preserve runnable examples, evidence, exercises and important qualifications
when rewriting. Rephrase or relocate them instead of silently deleting them.
Choose structure for the particular lesson. Do not invent graded questions or
worked examples to satisfy a schema. Reflections may be ungraded and diagrams
may be absent. Review the reader-facing result, including navigation and the
actual neighbouring chapters, before calling an editorial change complete.
